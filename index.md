# Shanka

Shanka puts a word from your deck on your lock screen and changes it every hour, or as often as you choose. You see it each time you check the time, so the words stay in front of you between study sessions.
{: .lead}

When you do sit down to study, a card shows only the word. Tap Easy if you know it, or reveal the meaning or hear it read aloud. Shanka keeps track of which part was hard, the meaning or the sound, and puts those words on the widget first.

Shanka also groups characters that share a sound part. 青 清 晴 請 all carry 青, and they sound alike in Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Vietnamese. Learning them as a group is easier than learning them one by one.

## Price

The first level is free, and the next one is free for early joiners: anyone who downloads in the first three months. A few sound groups are free too. Everything after that is a one-time pack: no subscription, no ads.
