# Shanka Hanzi: Support

Questions that come up most, and how to reach me if yours isn't here.

## Purchases

### I bought a pack but it's locked again. How do I get it back?

Open **Settings › Packs** and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with (iPhone Settings › your name › Media & Purchases). Purchases are one-time and never expire, so restoring is free, also after deleting the app or moving to a new device.

If Restore says nothing was found, check that the purchase shows up in your Apple Account's purchase history (iPhone Settings › your name › Media & Purchases › View Account › Purchase History).

### Does Family Sharing work?

Yes. Every pack, and Shanka Complete, can be shared with your Family Sharing group. Family members get it automatically once they install the app. If they don't see it, they can tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings › Packs. The person who bought it needs **Purchase Sharing** turned on (iPhone Settings › your name › Family Sharing › Purchase Sharing).

### Is there a subscription?

No. Each pack is bought once and is yours from then on. Shanka Complete is all four packs together, for less than the four bought one by one. Complete's price doesn't go down for packs you already own, so if you have some, compare it with buying just the ones you're missing.

### I have an offer code. Where do I enter it?

In the App Store app: tap your account picture at the top right, then **Redeem Gift Card or Code**, and type or scan the code. You can also open a redemption link if you were given one. The pack shows up in Shanka the next time you open it. If it doesn't, tap **Restore Purchases** in Settings › Packs.

### Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Audio

### There's no Cantonese voice, or the voice sounds robotic

Shanka reads cards with the voices built into iOS, and iOS doesn't always come with a Cantonese one installed. To add one:

1. Open the iPhone **Settings › Accessibility › Read & Speak › Voices**.
2. Tap **Chinese**, then pick a voice listed under Chinese (Hong Kong) and download it.
3. Download an Enhanced or Premium voice for a more natural sound. The same works for Mandarin under Chinese (China mainland) or Chinese (Taiwan).

Then pick the voice in Shanka under **Settings › Audio & voices**. On older iOS versions the menu is called Spoken Content instead of Read & Speak.

## The widget

### How do I put Shanka on the lock screen?

1. Touch and hold the lock screen until **Customize** appears, then tap it and choose **Lock Screen**.
2. Tap the widget area below the clock, and choose **Shanka**.
3. Pick a size, then tap **Done**.

There are also home screen widgets: touch and hold an empty spot on the home screen, tap **Edit › Add Widget** and search for Shanka.

What the widget shows is set in **Settings › Widget**: the deck, how often the word changes (from every 30 minutes to once a day), shuffle, the romanization, the script and traditional characters. Your weakest words come first, then new ones. On home screen widgets, the ↻ button moves to the next word. Lock screen widgets open the app when tapped.

### The widget isn't changing, or shows an old word

iOS decides exactly when widgets refresh, so a change can be a few minutes late. Opening Shanka once updates it straight away. If it stays blank, remove the widget and add it again.

## Decks and progress

### How do I import an Anki deck?

1. In Anki on a computer, choose **File › Export**, pick **Anki Deck Package (.apkg)**, and tick **Support older Anki versions**. Shanka can't read Anki's newest format yet.
2. Move the file to your iPhone, for example with AirDrop or iCloud Drive.
3. In Shanka, tap **+** in the library and choose the file, or open it from the Files app and share it to Shanka.

Shared decks are at [ankiweb.net/shared/decks](https://ankiweb.net/shared/decks). Importing an updated version of a deck you already have keeps your progress on the cards it shares with the old one.

### How do I back up my progress?

Open a deck's page and tap **Export progress**. It saves the deck's progress files (Mandarin, and Cantonese if you've studied it) wherever you pick, such as Files or AirDrop.

Shanka doesn't sync between devices and has no account, so this file and your device's iCloud or computer backup are the only copies. Restoring the whole device from a backup brings your progress back. The app doesn't yet have a button for reading an exported progress file back in. If you need to move progress to a new device that way, write to me.

### Is my data sent anywhere?

No. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
